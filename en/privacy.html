Privacy

How I handle your data.

Last updated November 6, 2026.

This site is operated by Luan Rodrigues Fotografia, based in Brazil. This page explains what data is collected through the Request a quote form, why, and for how long. Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) applies.

What is collected

When you submit a quote request, I store:

  • Name and email (required to reply).
  • WhatsApp (optional; used only if you fill it in).
  • Session type, preferred date, location, duration, styles, and references you described.
  • Any free notes you wrote.
  • IP address and browser user-agent (only to prevent spam and abuse).
  • Language in which you filled the wizard.

No tracking cookies. No sharing with advertisers.

Purpose

  • Reply to your request within 48h.
  • Prepare a quote and schedule.
  • Keep conversation history if you become a client.
  • Aggregate operational metrics (how many quotes came in, what type), never identifying you individually.

Who has access

Data is stored on my database provider (Supabase, South America servers). When enabled, automated notifications are sent through Resend. The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services process data necessary for their operation.

Retention

  • If no session happened: the request is deleted within 12 months after my last reply.
  • If you became a client: I keep the record while the relationship lasts and for up to 5 years after the last job, for tax and portfolio purposes.
  • You can request deletion sooner at any time.

Your rights (LGPD)

Any time you can request:

  • Confirmation that I process your data.
  • A copy of what I hold (portability).
  • Correction of incomplete or outdated data.
  • Anonymization, blocking, or deletion.
  • Consent withdrawal (the quote request is deleted).

Email rodfelluan@gmail.com with subject "LGPD". I reply within 15 calendar days.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. The database sits behind authentication and Row-Level Security. Only I read the quote content.

Minors

This form is not intended for minors under 18. If you are, please ask a guardian to email me instead.

Changes to this policy

Significant changes update this page and the date on top. Minor edits (wording, formatting) do not.

Browsing and preferences

We measure visits, pages and photos viewed, and active time, without an account or advertising cookies. A random identifier in sessionStorage distinguishes a session and is not used to recognize you on other sites. We store the referring domain and campaign parameters when present. We do not send your IP address to an external geolocation service.

localStorage keeps your likes and analytics opt-out preference. sessionStorage temporarily keeps the quote draft and session; you can clear these in your browser. You can request information or deletion using the contact below. The client portal uses a private link and temporary photo URLs; do not share the link.
